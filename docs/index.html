Opening it the first time

Mac

  1. Open QuranVideoMaker-Mac.dmg and drag the app into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. Your Mac will say it can't check it — that's because the app isn't from the App Store. Press Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and press Open Anyway next to Quran Video Maker. You only do this once.
  4. The app downloads its listener (about 480 MB, one time), then you're ready.

Windows

  1. Run QuranVideoMaker-Windows-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, press More info, then Run anyway. (It says that about any app that isn't from a big company.)
  3. Press Install. It opens by itself when it's done.
  4. The app downloads its listener (about 480 MB, one time), then you're ready.

Tip: name your recordings like Mulk7-12.mp3 or Qadr.mp3 and the app knows the ayahs by itself.
